# Liyamu privacy policy

Liyamu is a keyboard. A keyboard sees everything you type, so this is the whole policy:

- **Liyamu does not collect, store or send anything you type.** It has no network code. The Android app does
  not request the internet permission; the iOS keyboard does not ask for "Full Access".
- **No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no third-party code that phones home.**
- **Passwords** and other secure fields are typed as plain letters and are not looked at for suggestions.
- **What stays on your device:** your settings and the shortcuts you write yourself. They are kept in the
  app's own storage and are removed when you uninstall it.
- Word suggestions come from word lists built into the app. They are not personalised and nothing is learned
  from your typing. If learning from your own typing is ever added, it will be off until you turn it on, will
  stay on your device, and will have a delete button.

Liyamu is free software (GPL-3.0-or-later); the source is at https://github.com/pnfo/liyamu so this can be
checked.
