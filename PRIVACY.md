# Liyamu privacy policy

Liyamu is a keyboard. A keyboard sees everything you type, so this is the whole policy:

- **Liyamu does not send anything you type anywhere, and nobody else stores it.** It has no network code. The Android app does
  not request the internet permission; the iOS keyboard does not ask for "Full Access".
- **No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no third-party code that phones home.**
- **Passwords** and other secure fields are typed as plain letters and are not looked at for suggestions.
- **Voice typing (Android)** is not done by Liyamu. The microphone key only changes to the voice typing
  keyboard already on your phone (usually Google voice typing) and you come back when you finish. What you say
  is handled by that service under its own privacy policy; Liyamu has no microphone permission and never
  hears it. If you do not want this, do not tap the microphone.
- **What stays on your device:** your settings, the shortcuts you write yourself, and the words you picked
  from the suggestions (the letters you typed and the word you chose for them, so that it is offered first
  next time). They are kept in the app's own storage, never leave the device, and are removed when you
  uninstall it. Learning can be turned off in the settings, and "Forget what was learned" deletes the list.
  Nothing is learned in password fields.
- Other word suggestions come from word lists built into the app.

Liyamu is free software (GPL-3.0-or-later); the source is at https://github.com/pnfo/liyamu so this can be
checked.
